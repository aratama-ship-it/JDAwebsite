# WDC 2026 — Eight Background Concepts

## Shared production constraints

- 16:9 landscape website image, used unchanged for both the desktop hero and PICK UP card.
- Left 42–45% remains dark, simple, and low-contrast for the supplied WDC logo and HTML copy.
- Exactly one competition diabolo on the right, fully inside the frame, visual center near 77% width and 36% height.
- The diabolo must have a mechanically plausible, clean, symmetrical silhouette: two equal opposing concave cups, narrow cylindrical central axle, straight shared axis, equal rim thickness, no twisting or melting.
- No embedded text, logos, flags, people, hands, faces, sponsor marks, watermark, extra diabolo, yo-yo, or malformed equipment.
- Taiwan identity comes from setting, light, materials, vegetation, architecture, or landscape rather than written signage.

## 01 — Taipei Neon Night

Photorealistic cinematic Taipei skyline at blue hour, Taipei 101 visible but secondary, wet rooftops and restrained magenta/cyan light trails. Deep navy and charcoal palette. Glossy magenta competition diabolo.

## 02 — Temple Lantern Glow

Traditional Taiwanese temple courtyard at dusk, carved roof ornaments and red lantern glow on the right and distant background, dark calm wall/sky on the left. Warm vermilion and gold palette. Deep red competition diabolo with gold rim highlights.

## 03 — Jiufen Rain

Rainy Jiufen hillside alley and layered lanterns receding into mist, wet stone reflections, cinematic documentary realism. Left side reduced to dark fog and roof silhouette. Ruby-magenta diabolo with realistic rain highlights.

## 04 — Sun Moon Lake Dawn

Quiet Sun Moon Lake at dawn with layered blue mountains, pale mist and a subtle lakeside pavilion or small distant boat. Spacious, serene photographic minimalism. Teal-blue diabolo with a restrained coral highlight.

## 05 — Alishan Sunrise

Alishan sunrise above a sea of clouds, dark cedar silhouettes and a distant red heritage train curve used subtly. Left side remains dark mountain shadow. Amber-red diabolo lit by sunrise rim light.

## 06 — Taiwan Retro Print

Sophisticated 1960s-inspired Taiwanese travel-poster aesthetic, screen-print grain, simplified Taipei 101/temple roof/mountain layers without lettering. Dark indigo left field, coral, mustard and turquoise accents. Geometrically exact flat-rendered diabolo.

## 07 — Future Taipei Circuit

Elegant near-future Taipei at night, abstract transit lines and luminous grid geometry derived from the city, Taipei 101 as a restrained beacon. Premium cinematic 3D realism, not game UI. Black-blue left negative space. Polished cyan-magenta diabolo.

## 08 — Paper-cut and Ink Taiwan

Contemporary Taiwanese paper-cut and ink-wash fusion: layered mountain wash, temple roofline, plum blossom and soft lantern-light circles kept mainly to the right. Off-black/navy left field. Crisp folded-paper diabolo with exact symmetrical geometry, refined rather than childish.

Selected asset: `08-paper-cut-ink-taiwan-v3.webp`. The earlier drafts are retained; v3 makes the central axle and three-quarter competition-diabolo form clearer at PICK UP size.
